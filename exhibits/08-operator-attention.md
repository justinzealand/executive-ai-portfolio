# Operator attention — problem baseline (excerpt)

> The baseline's stated job: *preserve the condition to be solved so candidate solutions can be reviewed as provisional responses rather than quietly redefining the problem.* It is deliberately solution-hidden — it authorizes no architecture, plan, prototype, or charter amendment. It went through four solution-hidden Claude Code / OpenClaw review passes with zero open P0–P2 problem-framing findings before being presented for operator freeze.

## Problem

- **Operator-attention bottleneck.** The system can generate implementation faster than Justin can continuously reconstruct, supervise, and verify it. At the current scale, the operating model exposes session coordination, re-entry work, implementation chronology, status scanning, Git and lifecycle ceremony, dependency tracking, quality reconstruction, and recovery decisions to his conscious attention. Whether concurrency itself is a root cause, an amplifier, or simply poorly abstracted remains open.
- **Weak abstraction and evidence.** Implementation detail is often a compensating control rather than the real need. Justin reads minute details because the system has not made original intent, parent connection, material change, complete outcome, quality evidence, residual risk, operator need, next authorization, and follow-through trustworthy at his operating level.
- **Strategic work displaced by tactical supervision.** Justin is an architect-level career programmer who now builds through LLM implementers and wants to operate at product, user-experience, architecture, technology, priority, policy, and consequential-risk levels. Required tactical reconstruction crowds out that work and new product origination.
- **Continuity does not yet match concurrency.** More than twenty Claude Code sessions and more than twenty Codex sessions may be active at once, with frequent movement between tools and continued steering from a phone. Work can become waiting, difficult to re-enter, uncertainly complete, or detached from its parent outcome. Justin specifically needs continuity at three levels—product or initiative, session or full-chat context, and last turn or prompt—and currently loses the parent connection during some handoffs and re-entry.
- **The operating model depends too much on current availability.** Justin's available attention is expected to become materially more constrained when the operator's availability is materially reduced. The exact future daily limit is not measured. The system must remain useful under sharply reduced, intermittent, and substantially phone-based availability rather than depend on today's long desktop workday.
- **Quality confidence is expensive to reconstruct.** Passing tests, a plausible explanation, or an agent saying "done" does not establish that the intended full outcome was delivered, scope and expectations were preserved, architecture remains sound, residual risk is known, and recovery is credible.

The core problem is not "hide technical information." It is: **reduce system-required operator attention and tactical reconstruction while preserving product velocity, architectural understanding, scope fidelity, quality confidence, and operator control over consequential decisions.**

## Desired outcomes

- **Strategic operating level.** Justin spends most intentional work time on product direction, user experience, architecture, technology, priorities, and new product ideas rather than session and implementation supervision.
- **Delegated implementation lifecycle.** Agents own code, Git, verification, shipping, routine recovery, cross-runtime coordination, and lifecycle continuity end to end only where existing policy or a later explicit authorization grants that authority, and always within limits for reversibility, consequence, data integrity, validation, and recovery.
- **Fast decision-ready re-entry.** Justin can understand the product or initiative, the session or full-chat purpose and parent connection, the last meaningful turn, the current state, architecture impact, evidence, residual risk, and recommended next action without transcript archaeology.
- **Scope and expectation fidelity.** Material narrowing, expansion, substitution, or reinterpretation of the intended outcome is detected early, communicated clearly, and explicitly decided by Justin rather than normalized as implementation simplification.
- **Evidence-backed quality confidence.** Risk-proportionate, artifact-grounded evidence and independent challenge provide architect-level confidence without requiring routine code, Git, transcript, or raw-log inspection.
- **Architect-level implementation breadcrumbs.** Concise implementation breadcrumbs remain in the default narrative so Justin can follow the engineering story; filenames, Git mechanics, exhaustive chronology, and debugging detail remain available on demand or surface when materially relevant.
- **Terminal-outcome continuity.** Authorized work remains owned through the full product or operating outcome and does not silently stop after a triager, dispositioner, roadmap entry, prototype, or enabling component.
- **Lower operational failure burden.** The frequency, duration, consequence, and required operator handling of operational failures decline through a Charter-compliant mix of prevention, detection, containment, recovery, safe degradation, or repair—without this baseline selecting a control plane, dashboard, automation architecture, or specific self-healing mechanism.
- **Reduced-availability resilience.** The model remains effective when Justin's availability is sharply reduced, intermittent, and phone-heavy, and it can support safe work over hours, days, and eventually weeks.
- **Determinism without rigidity.** Deterministic protections bind data integrity, authority, evidence, and repeatable contracts while preserving LLM flexibility for ambiguous judgment, creative solution search, product exploration, and reversible iteration.
- **Malleable operating system.** Mechanisms remain replaceable and retireable when evidence shows a better path; the first implementation does not become permanent architecture by inertia.
- **Cross-runtime coherence.** Claude Code, Codex, and OpenClaw share the same operator-facing semantics, authority boundaries, evidence expectations, and named gaps so Justin does not have to remember accidental tool differences.
- **Future-domain portability.** Proven mechanisms can later support a parallel work-life domain without weakening the Platform Charter's personal-life stability gate, privacy boundary, or operator authority. This is an eventual portability outcome, not authorization or a current rollout promise.

## Method and findings

The baseline was grounded in a measurement of what the operator was actually being asked to do, not in an estimate.

- **Corpus.** Every prompt the operator had issued across Claude Code and Codex sessions in a **30-day window** was retained and classified — **4,419 operator prompts** after correction. An earlier raw count was materially higher and was **corrected** once it was found that Claude Code stores tool-result rows as `role=user` in its session logs, so a naive count of user-role rows had inflated the operator's apparent prompt volume with machine-generated tool output.
- **Manual review.** **240 samples** were reviewed by hand to calibrate the classification and to check what the categories were actually capturing.
- **Classification limits, reported.** The baseline's evidence record states that the semantic categories and the cross-runtime denominators have documented limitations and cannot by themselves choose an architecture or a permanent runtime role — the numbers are treated as directional, not decision-grade.
- **Headline finding.** Of the **4,230 machine-generated prompts** in the window, **3,266** were review, audit, or critique traffic — the system reviewing itself. The operator's own load was not dominated by writing code or steering product; it was dominated by coordination, re-entry, verification, and recovery ceremony that the system's own controls had generated.
- **Reframing.** The measurement moved the target. The goal is not fewer prompts, fewer sessions, or less concurrency — those are explicitly excluded as activity metrics — but **minimum human coordination per successful objective**. Voluntary technical engagement the operator chooses is not load to remove; system-required reconstruction is.

The raw corpus — prompt text, per-row classifications, and the operator's discovery answers — is **deliberately unpublished**. It contains the operator's working conversations and is referenced in the baseline only by content hash so that an approval can be bound to an exact evidence snapshot without reproducing it.
