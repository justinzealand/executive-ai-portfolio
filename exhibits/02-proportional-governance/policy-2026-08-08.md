# Proportional plan-review policy

**Status:** active workspace policy — operator decision 2026-08-08 (tiers);
amended 2026-09-02 (restoration: the v2 kernel is the review engine for the
Significant and Consequential tiers).

The v2 kernel is the review engine for significant and consequential plans;
Local plans get no packet and no gate. A packet frozen over bytes other than
the plan's current bytes is never authority. Use deterministic local checks for
ordinary work and the kernel's tiered review only when a plan is
architecture-significant (`Architecture-significant: Yes` or `Unknown`).

## Review levels

| Level | Applies to | Required review |
|---|---|---|
| Local | Read-only tools, documentation, diagnostics, and small internal reversible changes with no production, external, secret, schema, or persistent-runtime effect. | No packet, no gate: deterministic checks of the named files only. |
| Significant | Architecture, a shared runtime contract, or a material user-facing behavior change without a production/external/security boundary. | Repository-grounded plan review through the v2 kernel, Tier L (`cd <agent-skills>/skills/review-kernel && python3 -m engine.cli run-pass --plan <plan> --repo <repo> --tier tier_l ...`); implement after `authorize --check` returns authorized. |
| Consequential | Production/runtime lifecycle changes; external sends or integrations; authentication, secrets, or security; schema/data migrations; persistent services; irreversible operations; or a shared policy that changes every runtime. | Repository-grounded plan review through the v2 kernel, Tier S (`cd <agent-skills>/skills/review-kernel && python3 -m engine.cli run-pass --plan <plan> --repo <repo> --tier tier_s ...`); implement after `authorize --check` returns authorized. P0/P1 findings require evidence and block until fixed or explicitly operator-accepted. Name the executable guard, test, or mandatory consumer for every material invariant. |

## Rules that scale with risk

- Verify only the repository surfaces the plan actually names or affects. Do
  not demand rollout, rollback, lifecycle, parity, or operator-load proof for
  a local read-only tool with none of those effects.
- Cross-runtime parity applies to shared behavior and shared policy, not to a
  runtime-private helper. A change that touches another runtime's files still
  owns end-to-end parity for those touched surfaces.
- For consequential work, a P0/P1 finding must cite the relevant file,
  symbol, test, loaded path, contract, or reproducible mechanism. A promise
  to add a future test is not proof.
- A consequential P1/P2 may be accepted temporarily only with a named owner,
  an executable check or hard boundary, and an expiry or re-review trigger.
  Prose callouts alone do not close it.
- Repeated P3 findings, or a P3 that weakens an inherited invariant, are P2.

## Restoration (2026-09-02)

This policy does not weaken Charter tripwires or direct operator authority.
Continue to stop for a material architecture, security, schema, production,
external-effect, or irreversible decision.

By operator instruction on 2026-09-02, the v2 kernel is the review engine for
Significant and Consequential plans (plan
`docs/plans/plan-review-v2-restoration.md`; kernel lineage-1 SHIP packet
`pkt-0faf1a441f6e9d06`). The three tiers and their membership rules above are
unchanged; only the engine named for the two upper tiers changed. A plan is
Local iff its Guardrail block reads `Architecture-significant: No`; `Yes` or
`Unknown` is upper-tier.

Enforcement (hook-guard gating context, `pretool_deny`, lint-before-authorize)
is live only while `<state>/review-kernel/implement-gate.enabled`
exists. That file is written solely by
`scripts/activate_implement_gate.py --enable`, which refuses unless every
precondition named in the plan's `## Activation switch and rollback` passes.
Rollback is `scripts/activate_implement_gate.py --disable` (unlinks the file;
effective on the next tool call in every runtime), followed by the per-surface
reverts the plan lists.
